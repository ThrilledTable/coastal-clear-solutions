Why Oyster Bay Homes Need Regular Window Cleaning

Oyster Bay is a historic hamlet centered on Oyster Bay Harbor, on the Long Island Sound, with a maritime heritage and a walkable downtown near the harborfront. The area is well known as the home of Sagamore Hill, Theodore Roosevelt's estate on Cove Neck overlooking the harbor — one example of the many waterfront and near-waterfront homes throughout the area. Homes close to the harbor and Sound pick up salt air and humidity that leaves windows hazy faster than homes further inland, and older homes with original multi-pane windows benefit from careful hand-detailed cleaning.
