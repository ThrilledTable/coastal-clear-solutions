Why Huntington Homes Need Regular Window Cleaning

Huntington sits on Long Island's North Shore around five protected harbors leading out to Long Island Sound — Huntington Bay, Lloyd Harbor, Centerport, Halesite, and Asharoken all front the water. Homes near the harbor and Sound-facing properties pick up salt air and moisture that leaves windows hazy and streaked faster than homes further inland. Huntington's mix of historic colonial-era homes and waterfront properties often means original window frames and multi-pane glass that benefit from careful, hand-detailed cleaning rather than a quick wipe-down.
