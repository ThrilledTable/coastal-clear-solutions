Why Bayville Homes Need Regular Window Cleaning

Bayville is a waterfront village nestled directly along Long Island Sound and Oyster Bay Harbor, with a history rooted in fishing and oystering that still shapes its maritime character today. Because Bayville homes sit right on the open Sound rather than tucked into a sheltered harbor, they're exposed to more direct salt spray and wind-driven moisture — which builds up on glass faster than in more protected communities. Regular cleaning keeps windows clear for the water views that make Bayville homes special.
