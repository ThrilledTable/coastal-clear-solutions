Why Cold Spring Harbor Homes Need Regular Window Cleaning

Cold Spring Harbor is a small, historic hamlet on Long Island's North Shore, once one of Long Island's most active whaling ports and today known for its quiet, timeless coastal village character. It sits right on the border of the Huntington and Oyster Bay harbor communities. Homes here — many with genuine historic character — sit close enough to the water that windows pick up salt air and moisture that dulls glass faster than homes set further inland, and older window frames benefit from careful, hand-detailed cleaning rather than a quick wipe-down.
