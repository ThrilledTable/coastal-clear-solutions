Why Centerport Homes Need Regular Window Cleaning

Centerport is a hamlet in the Town of Huntington set on Centerport Harbor, one of the protected harbors that lead out to Long Island Sound. Its quieter, smaller-scale waterfront character sits between the busier villages of Huntington and Northport. Homes near the harbor pick up salt air and moisture that dulls glass faster than homes further inland, making regular cleaning worthwhile for keeping water views clear.
