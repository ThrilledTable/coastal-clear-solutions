Why Northport Homes Need Regular Window Cleaning

Northport is a village set directly on Northport Harbor near Long Island Sound, with a walkable Main Street and a historic waterfront that dates back to the village's oyster-fishing era. Homes here range from Victorian-era and turn-of-the-century houses near the harborfront to updated waterfront properties — many with original multi-pane windows that need careful, hand-detailed cleaning. Harborfront homes and those near the water pick up moisture and salt air that leaves glass hazy faster than homes set further back from the Sound.
