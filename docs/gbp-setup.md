# Google Business Profile Setup — SoundView Window Care

Concrete steps for creating and launching the GBP listing. This is the
highest-leverage action identified in the initial lead-gen pass — GBP is
typically the top lead source for local service businesses, and this
business currently has no listing at all.

## 1. Start the listing
At business.google.com → "Add your business"
- Business name: **SoundView Window Care**
- Business type: **Service Area Business** (no storefront) — check "I
  deliver goods and services to my customers" and hide the address.

## 2. Categories
- Primary: **Window Cleaning Service**
- Secondary (only if actually offered): Pressure Washing Service, Gutter
  Cleaning Service — don't add categories for services not actually
  provided.

## 3. Service areas
List specific towns, not just "Long Island" — Google weights named areas
higher than county-level entries. Starting candidates (trim to actual
coverage): Huntington, Long Beach, Port Washington, Garden City, Great
Neck, Babylon, Islip, Smithtown, Hempstead.

## 4. Business description (750 char max)
Include: "residential window cleaning," "screen/track/frame cleaning,"
2-3 town names, and one genuine differentiator (e.g. the site's instant
online quote calculator). No keyword stuffing.

## 5. Photos
Upload 10-15 before publishing: logo, cover photo, team/equipment/van
photos, before/after shots if available. Zero-photo listings get far
fewer map-pack clicks regardless of category accuracy.

## 6. Booking link
Add the Calendly URL as the GBP "Book" / appointment link — the most
direct path from search to a booked job.

## 7. Q&A
Seed 3-5 owner-answered questions: service area coverage, how pricing
works (point to the site's quote calculator), whether storm windows are
included, insurance/licensing if applicable.

## 8. First Google Post
Publish one the week of launch (an offer, a tip, or a service-area
announcement) — signals activity, which matters most with zero reviews.

## 9. Reviews
Standing habit from day one: request a review the day a job finishes,
while satisfaction is freshest. Review count/velocity is one of the
biggest local-pack ranking factors, and this business starts at zero
against competitors with hundreds.

## Not automatable
Creating and verifying the actual listing requires the owner's Google
account — this checklist is what to fill in once inside the GBP
dashboard, not something a subagent can do directly.
